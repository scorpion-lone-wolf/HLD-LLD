# Progress & Evidence

## Unified evidence scale

| Level | Status | Meaning |
|---:|---|---|
| U | unassessed | no evidence |
| 0 | unfamiliar | cannot yet explain/use |
| 1 | learning | recognizes after explanation |
| 2 | practicing | uses with guidance |
| 3 | verified | independently applies to a familiar problem |
| 4 | transfer-verified | independently applies to an unfamiliar problem and defends trade-offs |
| R | revisit | prior knowledge needs refresh |

Topic verification does not mean domain interview-ready.

## Rules

- Never infer positive evidence from job title or years of experience.
- Never mark Level 3+ because a lesson was merely completed.
- Level 4 requires unfamiliar transfer plus trade-off defense.
- A failed retention check may move a topic to `revisit`.
